# Warriors Gym — Official Website Demo

A modern, high-performance combat gym and fitness facility website designed for **Warriors Gym** located on Al Maha St, Bawshar, Muscat, Oman.

## Features

- **Cinematic Combat Intro**: High-impact combat hype animation with audio-visual climax reveal and transparent logo branding (runs once per session; skippable on click, tap, or ESC).
- **Mobile Responsive & Device Adaptive**: Fully optimized for smartphones, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide screens.
- **5-Panel Interactive Expanding Accordion**:
  - Horizontal expanding deck on desktop.
  - Interactive touch vertical card deck on mobile devices for training disciplines and coaching staff.
- **Tabbed Weekly Timetable ("Plan Your Week")**: Interactive schedule (Saturday–Thursday, closed Fridays), including youth combat, ladies-only classes, fitness box, kickboxing, and western boxing.
- **Touch-Swipe Action Slideshow**: Interactive gallery with smooth slide transitions, dot indicators, arrow navigation, and touch-swipe gestures.
- **Direct WhatsApp Integration**:
  - Pre-filled structured enquiry form sending client details and selected time slots directly to WhatsApp number `+968 9222 3330`.
  - Floating WhatsApp action button with safe-area spacing.
- **Typography & Styling**: Clean athletic aesthetic utilizing Google Fonts (`Bebas Neue` & `Barlow Condensed`), zero emojis, and FontAwesome icons throughout.

## Pages

1. `index.html` — Homepage featuring the cinematic combat intro, hero banner, interactive disciplines deck, coaching roster, schedule preview, pricing table, and testimonials.
2. `about.html` — Philosophy, warrior code, facility background, and head coaches.
3. `programs.html` — Detailed disciplines breakdown (Boxing, Muay Thai, Kick Boxing, Fitness Box, Kids Combat, Ladies-Only).
4. `schedule.html` — Full day-by-day timetable from Saturday through Thursday.
5. `gallery.html` — Interactive touch-swipe action slideshow and photography grid.
6. `contact.html` — Facility address, admissions contacts, direct WhatsApp booking form, and embedded Google Maps pin.

## Tech Stack

- **HTML5 & CSS3**: Vanilla responsive layouts using Flexbox, CSS Grid, and custom properties.
- **JavaScript (ES6+)**: Custom touch handlers, swipe detection, accordion toggle, session management, and WhatsApp URL generation without external dependencies.
- **Icons**: FontAwesome 6 (CDN).
- **Fonts**: Google Fonts (`Bebas Neue`, `Barlow`, `Barlow Condensed`).
